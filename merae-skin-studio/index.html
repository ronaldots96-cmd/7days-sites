Questions, welcomed

A few things worth
knowing first.

Thoughtful care makes room for questions, alternatives and the option to wait.

Do I need to know which treatment I want?

No. A consultation can begin with a goal or question. The appropriate next step may be a treatment, a skincare plan or no treatment at all.

What happens during a consultation?

A real provider would review your goals, relevant history, suitability, alternatives, risks, expected downtime and pricing before making a recommendation.

Are results guaranteed?

No aesthetic result can be guaranteed. Suitability, response and downtime vary from person to person.

How should I prepare?

A live clinic would provide individualized instructions after reviewing your request. Do not stop medication or change medical care without guidance from a qualified clinician.

Is Merae a real clinic?

No. Merae Skin Studio is a fictional website concept created to demonstrate strategy and design for medical-aesthetics practices. It does not provide medical services or accept appointments.
